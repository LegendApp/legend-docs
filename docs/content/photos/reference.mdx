Add folders in Library Settings to browse `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.heic`, and `.webp` files. The app scans subfolders and watches configured folders for changes. Apple Photos albums appear in the sidebar after permission is granted; each album currently loads up to 1,000 items.

Default shortcuts are the arrow keys to navigate the grid, `-` / `=` to adjust columns, `Enter` to open the selected photo, and `Escape` or `Enter` to close its fullscreen view. Press `F` in fullscreen to toggle the filmstrip, `S` in the grid to toggle the sidebar, and `/` to show shortcut help. Shortcuts can be changed in Settings.

Use `1`–`5` to rate a photo and `0` to clear its rating. `Space` or `P` toggles a flag, and `X` toggles rejection. Ratings, flags, and rejection status are saved in the app’s `metadata.json` file.
