Legend Chat History discovers local transcripts in `~/.codex/sessions`, `~/.codex/archived_sessions`, and `~/.claude/projects`, groups them by provider, and remembers your last selection.

The composer streams simulated responses in memory. It sends nothing to a model and does not save generated messages.
