Legend Chat History requires macOS 14 or later and supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

Choose the Legend Chat History `-arm.zip` archive for Apple silicon or `-x86.zip` for Intel Macs. Unzip it and move the app to Applications. Release builds support automatic updates through Sparkle.

This is an example for exploring the underlying technology.
